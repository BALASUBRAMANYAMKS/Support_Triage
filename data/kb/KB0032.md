# VPN: VPN disconnects every few minutes while working from home: guided fix

## Symptoms
The same issue is affecting a work task: VPN disconnects every few minutes while working from home, while supporting a time-sensitive team task.

## Cause
the client version has a known idle-timeout defect

## Resolution
1. upgrade the managed VPN client to the approved version and verify stability
2. Confirm the service works from the affected device or account.
3. Record what changed and whether the issue returned.

## When to raise a ticket
Raise a ticket if these steps fail, the issue affects multiple people, or the service is business-critical. Include the exact error and affected device or app.
