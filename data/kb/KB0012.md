# Teams: Teams calls have no audio after a headset reconnects: guided fix

## Symptoms
Since this morning, Teams calls have no audio after a headset reconnects; the employee has already signed out and back in.

## Cause
the call selected a disconnected audio endpoint

## Resolution
1. select the active headset and place a test call
2. Confirm the service works from the affected device or account.
3. Record what changed and whether the issue returned.

## When to raise a ticket
Raise a ticket if these steps fail, the issue affects multiple people, or the service is business-critical. Include the exact error and affected device or app.
