# Installation / Licence: Power BI installation reports that no licence is available: guided fix

## Symptoms
Since this morning, Power BI installation reports that no licence is available; the employee has already signed out and back in.

## Cause
the user is not in the licensed security group

## Resolution
1. confirm entitlement approval and assign the licence group
2. Confirm the service works from the affected device or account.
3. Record what changed and whether the issue returned.

## When to raise a ticket
Raise a ticket if these steps fail, the issue affects multiple people, or the service is business-critical. Include the exact error and affected device or app.
