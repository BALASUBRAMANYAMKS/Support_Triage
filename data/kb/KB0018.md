# Monitor & Peripherals: an external monitor flickers through the docking station: guided fix

## Symptoms
Since this morning, an external monitor flickers through the docking station; the employee has already signed out and back in.

## Cause
dock firmware and display refresh settings are incompatible

## Resolution
1. apply approved dock firmware and use the supported refresh rate
2. Confirm the service works from the affected device or account.
3. Record what changed and whether the issue returned.

## When to raise a ticket
Raise a ticket if these steps fail, the issue affects multiple people, or the service is business-critical. Include the exact error and affected device or app.
