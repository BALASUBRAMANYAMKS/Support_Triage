# New Hardware Request: a new joiner needs a standard laptop before the start date: guided fix

## Symptoms
The same issue is affecting a work task: a new joiner needs a standard laptop before the start date, while supporting a time-sensitive team task.

## Cause
the approved request is waiting for asset allocation

## Resolution
1. confirm approval, allocate a standard device and record its asset tag
2. Confirm the service works from the affected device or account.
3. Record what changed and whether the issue returned.

## When to raise a ticket
Raise a ticket if these steps fail, the issue affects multiple people, or the service is business-critical. Include the exact error and affected device or app.
