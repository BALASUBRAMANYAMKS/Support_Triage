# Mobile Device: a managed phone no longer receives work-profile notifications: guided fix

## Symptoms
Since this morning, a managed phone no longer receives work-profile notifications; the employee has already signed out and back in.

## Cause
notification permission reset after an OS update

## Resolution
1. restore the approved work-profile setting and send a test alert
2. Confirm the service works from the affected device or account.
3. Record what changed and whether the issue returned.

## When to raise a ticket
Raise a ticket if these steps fail, the issue affects multiple people, or the service is business-critical. Include the exact error and affected device or app.
