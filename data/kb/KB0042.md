# Database: a reporting query times out for several analysts: guided fix

## Symptoms
Since this morning, a reporting query times out for several analysts; the employee has already signed out and back in.

## Cause
scheduled index maintenance is holding a long-running lock

## Resolution
1. inspect the blocking session and reschedule maintenance with the database owner
2. Confirm the service works from the affected device or account.
3. Record what changed and whether the issue returned.

## When to raise a ticket
Raise a ticket if these steps fail, the issue affects multiple people, or the service is business-critical. Include the exact error and affected device or app.
