# Phishing Report: an employee received a message asking them to approve an unexpected sign-in: guided fix

## Symptoms
The same issue is affecting a work task: an employee received a message asking them to approve an unexpected sign-in, while supporting a time-sensitive team task.

## Cause
the message uses a lookalike sender domain to harvest credentials

## Resolution
1. do not click links, report the message, and notify Security Operations
2. Confirm the service works from the affected device or account.
3. Record what changed and whether the issue returned.

## When to raise a ticket
Raise a ticket if these steps fail, the issue affects multiple people, or the service is business-critical. Include the exact error and affected device or app.
