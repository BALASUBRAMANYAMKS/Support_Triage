# Distribution List: removed employee still receives announcements: guided fix

## Symptoms
An employee removed from an internal distribution list still receives its announcements the next day.

## Cause
The distribution-list membership cache has not refreshed after the approved removal.

## Resolution
1. Verify the approved removal, refresh the directory membership state, and confirm the employee no longer receives a test message.
2. Confirm the service works from the affected device or account.
3. Record what changed and whether the issue returned.

## When to raise a ticket
Raise a ticket if these steps fail, the issue affects multiple people, or the service is business-critical. Include the exact error and affected device or app.
