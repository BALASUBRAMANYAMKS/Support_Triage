# DNS / Proxy: an approved vendor portal returns a proxy resolution error: guided fix

## Symptoms
Since this morning, an approved vendor portal returns a proxy resolution error; the employee has already signed out and back in.

## Cause
the vendor hostname is missing from the managed allow-list

## Resolution
1. validate business-owner approval and update the managed allow-list
2. Confirm the service works from the affected device or account.
3. Record what changed and whether the issue returned.

## When to raise a ticket
Raise a ticket if these steps fail, the issue affects multiple people, or the service is business-critical. Include the exact error and affected device or app.
