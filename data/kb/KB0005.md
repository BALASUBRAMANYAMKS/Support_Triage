# Unexpected repeated MFA prompts while idle: guided fix

## Symptoms
An employee receives repeated sign-in approval prompts while not attempting to log in.

## Cause
Someone may have the employee password and is triggering MFA prompts in an attempt to gain access.

## Resolution
1. Tell the employee not to approve prompts, report the event to Security Operations, and follow the approved credential reset and session revocation process.
2. Confirm the service works from the affected device or account.
3. Record what changed and whether the issue returned.

## When to raise a ticket
Raise a ticket if these steps fail, the issue affects multiple people, or the service is business-critical. Include the exact error and affected device or app.
