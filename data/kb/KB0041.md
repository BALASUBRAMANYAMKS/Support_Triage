# Backup & Restore: the nightly backup job reports a failed snapshot: guided fix

## Symptoms
The same issue is affecting a work task: the nightly backup job reports a failed snapshot, while supporting a time-sensitive team task.

## Cause
the snapshot window overlapped a storage maintenance task

## Resolution
1. confirm maintenance completed and rerun the approved backup job
2. Confirm the service works from the affected device or account.
3. Record what changed and whether the issue returned.

## When to raise a ticket
Raise a ticket if these steps fail, the issue affects multiple people, or the service is business-critical. Include the exact error and affected device or app.
