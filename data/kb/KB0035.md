# Wired LAN / Internet: a desk has no wired network after a floor move: guided fix

## Symptoms
The same issue is affecting a work task: a desk has no wired network after a floor move, while supporting a time-sensitive team task.

## Cause
the desk port is patched to a deactivated switch port

## Resolution
1. verify port mapping and activate the approved switch port
2. Confirm the service works from the affected device or account.
3. Record what changed and whether the issue returned.

## When to raise a ticket
Raise a ticket if these steps fail, the issue affects multiple people, or the service is business-critical. Include the exact error and affected device or app.
