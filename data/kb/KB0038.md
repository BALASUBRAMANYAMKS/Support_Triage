# Server / VM: a scheduled application VM is not responding after maintenance: guided fix

## Symptoms
The same issue is affecting a work task: a scheduled application VM is not responding after maintenance, while supporting a time-sensitive team task.

## Cause
the VM service did not restart after its maintenance window

## Resolution
1. check the service event log and restart the approved service
2. Confirm the service works from the affected device or account.
3. Record what changed and whether the issue returned.

## When to raise a ticket
Raise a ticket if these steps fail, the issue affects multiple people, or the service is business-critical. Include the exact error and affected device or app.
