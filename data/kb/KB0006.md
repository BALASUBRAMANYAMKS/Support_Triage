# New Access Request: new hire needs read-only access to the onboarding site: guided fix

## Symptoms
A new hire needs read-only access to the Finance onboarding workspace in SAP before their first reporting cycle.

## Cause
The manager approval is recorded, but the requested read-only group was not included in the provisioning task.

## Resolution
1. Confirm the approved access scope, add the user to the read-only group, and verify the site opens.
2. Confirm the service works from the affected device or account.
3. Record what changed and whether the issue returned.

## When to raise a ticket
Raise a ticket if these steps fail, the issue affects multiple people, or the service is business-critical. Include the exact error and affected device or app.
