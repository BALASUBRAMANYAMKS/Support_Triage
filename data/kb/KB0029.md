# Browser: an internal portal loops back to sign-in in Chrome: guided fix

## Symptoms
The same issue is affecting a work task: an internal portal loops back to sign-in in Chrome, while supporting a time-sensitive team task.

## Cause
a stale single-sign-on cookie is being reused

## Resolution
1. clear portal site data and retry with the managed browser profile
2. Confirm the service works from the affected device or account.
3. Record what changed and whether the issue returned.

## When to raise a ticket
Raise a ticket if these steps fail, the issue affects multiple people, or the service is business-critical. Include the exact error and affected device or app.
