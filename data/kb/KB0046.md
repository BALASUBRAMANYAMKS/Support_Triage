# Lost or Stolen Device: a managed phone was lost during travel and contains work data: guided fix

## Symptoms
a managed phone was lost during travel and contains work data.

## Cause
the device is outside the employee control

## Resolution
1. report it immediately, lock it through the approved portal, and notify Security Operations
2. Confirm the service works from the affected device or account.
3. Record what changed and whether the issue returned.

## When to raise a ticket
Raise a ticket if these steps fail, the issue affects multiple people, or the service is business-critical. Include the exact error and affected device or app.
