# MFA: the authenticator prompt does not arrive on the registered phone: guided fix

## Symptoms
the authenticator prompt does not arrive on the registered phone.

## Cause
the device registration is out of sync with the identity tenant

## Resolution
1. verify the registered device and re-enrol MFA through self-service
2. Confirm the service works from the affected device or account.
3. Record what changed and whether the issue returned.

## When to raise a ticket
Raise a ticket if these steps fail, the issue affects multiple people, or the service is business-critical. Include the exact error and affected device or app.
