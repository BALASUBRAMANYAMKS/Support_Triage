# Storage / File Share: the shared finance drive is unavailable to the whole team: guided fix

## Symptoms
Since this morning, the shared finance drive is unavailable to the whole team; the employee has already signed out and back in.

## Cause
the file-share volume reached its capacity threshold

## Resolution
1. restore approved capacity and verify access from two team accounts
2. Confirm the service works from the affected device or account.
3. Record what changed and whether the issue returned.

## When to raise a ticket
Raise a ticket if these steps fail, the issue affects multiple people, or the service is business-critical. Include the exact error and affected device or app.
