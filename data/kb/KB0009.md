# Outlook: messages remain stuck in the Outbox: guided fix

## Symptoms
Messages with attachments remain stuck in the Outlook Outbox while other mail is received normally.

## Cause
Outlook is working offline or repeatedly retrying an attachment larger than the allowed message size.

## Resolution
1. Check connection state and the message-size limit; remove or reduce the oversized attachment and resend.
2. Confirm the service works from the affected device or account.
3. Record what changed and whether the issue returned.

## When to raise a ticket
Raise a ticket if these steps fail, the issue affects multiple people, or the service is business-critical. Include the exact error and affected device or app.
