# Mailbox / Quota: mail cannot be sent because the mailbox is over quota: guided fix

## Symptoms
The same issue is affecting a work task: mail cannot be sent because the mailbox is over quota, while supporting a time-sensitive team task.

## Cause
large retained attachments consumed the mailbox quota

## Resolution
1. archive or remove approved large items and confirm quota reduction
2. Confirm the service works from the affected device or account.
3. Record what changed and whether the issue returned.

## When to raise a ticket
Raise a ticket if these steps fail, the issue affects multiple people, or the service is business-critical. Include the exact error and affected device or app.
