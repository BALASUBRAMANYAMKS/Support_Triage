# Laptop / Desktop: a laptop battery drains rapidly during video calls: guided fix

## Symptoms
The same issue is affecting a work task: a laptop battery drains rapidly during video calls, while supporting a time-sensitive team task.

## Cause
a background conferencing process keeps the graphics device active

## Resolution
1. apply the approved driver update and check battery use
2. Confirm the service works from the affected device or account.
3. Record what changed and whether the issue returned.

## When to raise a ticket
Raise a ticket if these steps fail, the issue affects multiple people, or the service is business-critical. Include the exact error and affected device or app.
