# Distribution List: an approved distribution-list change is not visible to a new member: guided fix

## Symptoms
Since this morning, an approved distribution-list change is not visible to a new member; the employee has already signed out and back in.

## Cause
the directory membership cache has not refreshed

## Resolution
1. confirm approval and allow directory replication to complete
2. Confirm the service works from the affected device or account.
3. Record what changed and whether the issue returned.

## When to raise a ticket
Raise a ticket if these steps fail, the issue affects multiple people, or the service is business-critical. Include the exact error and affected device or app.
