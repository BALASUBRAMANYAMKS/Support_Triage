# Password Reset: new password rejected after reset: guided fix

## Symptoms
The reset portal accepts a new password, but the next sign-in rejects it.

## Cause
The new password conflicts with the current password-history or complexity policy.

## Resolution
1. Check the current password policy and guide the employee through a fresh reset using a unique compliant passphrase; never request the passphrase.
2. Confirm the service works from the affected device or account.
3. Record what changed and whether the issue returned.

## When to raise a ticket
Raise a ticket if these steps fail, the issue affects multiple people, or the service is business-critical. Include the exact error and affected device or app.
