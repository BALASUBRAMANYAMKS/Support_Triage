# Permission Change: former team access remains after department transfer: guided fix

## Symptoms
After moving to another department, an employee can still open the former team share.

## Cause
The previous department security group was not removed from the directory account.

## Resolution
1. Verify the transfer approval, remove the obsolete group membership, and confirm access is denied.
2. Confirm the service works from the affected device or account.
3. Record what changed and whether the issue returned.

## When to raise a ticket
Raise a ticket if these steps fail, the issue affects multiple people, or the service is business-critical. Include the exact error and affected device or app.
