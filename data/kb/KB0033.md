# Wi-Fi: several meeting-room devices cannot join employee Wi-Fi: guided fix

## Symptoms
Since this morning, several meeting-room devices cannot join employee Wi-Fi; the employee has already signed out and back in.

## Cause
the room access point has stale client associations

## Resolution
1. refresh the access-point client table and verify a test connection
2. Confirm the service works from the affected device or account.
3. Record what changed and whether the issue returned.

## When to raise a ticket
Raise a ticket if these steps fail, the issue affects multiple people, or the service is business-critical. Include the exact error and affected device or app.
