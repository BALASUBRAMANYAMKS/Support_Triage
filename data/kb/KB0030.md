# OS Update: a managed Windows update repeatedly rolls back: guided fix

## Symptoms
Since this morning, a managed Windows update repeatedly rolls back; the employee has already signed out and back in.

## Cause
the device lacks free disk space for update staging

## Resolution
1. free approved disk space and retry the managed update
2. Confirm the service works from the affected device or account.
3. Record what changed and whether the issue returned.

## When to raise a ticket
Raise a ticket if these steps fail, the issue affects multiple people, or the service is business-critical. Include the exact error and affected device or app.
