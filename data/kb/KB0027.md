# Business Application: SAP FICO rejects a valid monthly-close transaction: guided fix

## Symptoms
Since this morning, SAP FICO rejects a valid monthly-close transaction; the employee has already signed out and back in.

## Cause
the current role lacks the approved posting-period permission

## Resolution
1. check the posting period and route an approved role update to the SAP owner
2. Confirm the service works from the affected device or account.
3. Record what changed and whether the issue returned.

## When to raise a ticket
Raise a ticket if these steps fail, the issue affects multiple people, or the service is business-critical. Include the exact error and affected device or app.
